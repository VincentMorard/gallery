Vincent Morard — acrylic painter

The Artist

Biography

Vincent Morard is an acrylic painter whose work celebrates light, colour and wide-open spaces. A passionate self-taught artist, he has spent years developing a personal pictorial language, somewhere between sensitive figuration and expressive momentum.

His canvases travel from alpine valleys to starry skies, from silent deserts to imaginary worlds, with one constant: the emotion of the captured moment.

Artistic approach

Every painting is born of an emotion: the freshness of a waterfall, the majesty of a summit, the energy of a gesture. Acrylic, with its vividness and directness, captures that intensity without compromise.

Composition favours breathing room: broad fields of colour, bold contrasts, and always a point of light guiding the eye.

Inspiration

Mountains hold a central place — the Tour du Mont-Blanc, alpine pastures, snowy peaks. But inspiration also comes from starry nights, wildlife, and worlds of fiction that widen the field of the possible.

Creative process

Everything begins with sketches and colour studies. The canvas is then built up in successive layers: backgrounds first, broad and instinctive, then structures, and finally the details and bursts of light that bring the scene to life.

Each work is painted by hand, signed, and accompanied by its authenticity details.
